Modern JavaScript-based sites (React, Next.js, SPA) are often "opaque" to AI crawlers, causing latency or erroneous indexing. CatyAI GEO Gateway acts as an interoperability layer between your content and AI engines.

Machine-Readable KB Index

/llms.txt

CatyAI automatically generates a standardized llms.txt file following emerging specifications for LLMs. This file serves a compressed Markdown index, optimized for the context window of GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, and Gemini Pro models.

Auto-generated llms.txt compliant Context-optimized

Real-time Bot Recognition

Our system identifies in real-time over 9 unique AI crawler signatures (OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity, Google, Microsoft, Meta, ByteDance, Cohere, Apple). Upon detection, the Gateway performs a digital "handshake", serving pre-rendered data and eliminating the need for heavy JS execution.

9+ AI signatures Real-time detection Pre-rendered serving

The /answer Portal

This is the direct access point. Instead of another AI "guessing" what's written on your site, it can query our /geo/v1/answer endpoint directly. Output: Structured JSON-LD + pure Markdown. Benefit: Zero hallucinations. The AI receives the truth directly from the source.
